summary

Hello. I'm yotio. I'm a software developer in Japan.
I was born in Machida City, Tokyo[1] Japan in 2001.
I started programming when I was in junior high school and opened YotioSoft in 2014 as a place to publish my works.
Since then, I have been hobby developing and releasing software for more than 10 years.
Initially, I was interested in the GUI of an OS and developed an OS-mock applications [2],
I then studied computer science at a technical college and an university where I became interested in the OS kernel itself.
In recent years, I have focused on the development of CLI tools and web applications.
[1] Some say Machida City is in Kanagawa Prefecture, not Tokyo. Sometimes Japanese people say this way as a joke.
[2] Launcher software that mimics the GUI of an OS such as Windows.
